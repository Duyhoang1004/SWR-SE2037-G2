# Literature Review Matrix

| Paper | Domain | AI Model / Method | Dataset | Evaluation Metrics | Main Contribution | Limitation | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Surve (2022) | E-Commerce / Product Classification | Transfer Learning – CNN, VGG19, InceptionV3, ResNet50, MobileNet (compared) | Flipkart E-Commerce Dataset (Kaggle) – 16,679 images after augmentation, 15 categories | Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, Inference Time | Benchmarked 5 models; MobileNet and InceptionV3 achieved best accuracy (85%) with MobileNet being fastest (0.101s/image); Transfer Learning outperforms CNN from scratch by 34% | Image only – no text input; small dataset (15 categories); tested on Flipkart only; hyperparameters not fully optimized | High |
| Jha et al. (2021) | E-Commerce / Fashion Product Classification | Transfer Learning – VGG-19, Inception V3 | Fashion MNIST – 70,000 grayscale 28×28 images, 10 fashion categories | Accuracy, Loss | Transfer Learning (VGG-19, Inception V3) outperforms CNN from scratch; faster convergence, less overfitting on fashion product images | Only grayscale 28×28 images – unrealistic for real e-commerce; large models unsuitable for mobile/edge; tested on fashion only | High |
| Khanuja (2019) | E-Commerce / Multi-category Product Classification | Transfer Learning – VGG-16 (ImageNet), One vs All binary classification strategy | ~17,500 self-crawled product images; 5 categories (Appliances, Electronics, Furniture, Toys + 1) | Accuracy, Loss, Training Time | One vs All TL (VGG-16) achieves ~89% accuracy vs ~35.9% multi-class TL and ~79% CNN from scratch; ~10x faster training (16 min vs 3+ hr) | Image only – no text input; One vs All not scalable to 100+ categories; no Precision/Recall/F1; dataset not public | High |
| Benson et al. (2024) | Smart Healthcare / Medical Diagnosis | Custom CNN, ResNet50 (Fine-tuned), Grad-CAM (XAI) | Chest X-Ray Images (Pneumonia) Dataset – 5,856 digital chest X-ray images, 3 categories | Accuracy, Sensitivity (Recall), F1-Score, Inference Latency | ResNet50 achieved 94.6% accuracy and 96.2% sensitivity, reducing missed cases to <4%; integrated Grad-CAM for explainable AI medical validation | Age-restricted data (pediatric patients 1-5yo only); lowers accuracy on adults; lacks clinical context integration (symptoms, lab data) | Medium |
| Sato & Nguyen (2025) | Smart Agriculture / Drone-based Plant Disease Detection | YOLOv8-Small (Detection) + MobileNetV3 (Classification) | Drone Imagery & PlantVillage – 12,400 high-quality RGB images, 4 categories | mAP@0.5, FPS (Frames Per Second), Model Size (MB) | Light-weight edge deployment pipeline (<40MB) running real-time at >45 FPS; YOLOv8 maps bounding boxes and MobileNetV3 classifies disease types | Highly vulnerable to weather and lighting variations; false positives increase on windy/cloudy days; no automated robotic treatment execution | Medium |
