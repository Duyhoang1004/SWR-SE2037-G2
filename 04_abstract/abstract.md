# Abstract

**[Context]** Automatically categorizing products using Deep Learning models like Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) has become essential in e-commerce to reduce manual labor for merchants and enhance user search experiences. 

**[Problem]** However, deploying these AI-based systems in production environments often fails due to a severe mismatch in traditional Requirements Engineering (RE) practices, which cannot handle the data-driven and non-deterministic nature of machine learning. Furthermore, there is a prominent research gap concerning empirical case studies in the e-commerce domain, specific guidance for eliciting non-functional requirements (NFRs) like explainability for "black-box" models, and structured Human-in-the-Loop mechanisms. 

**[Objective]** This study aims to adapt and validate a structured RE framework tailored for an AI-based e-commerce product categorization system, successfully balancing algorithmic accuracy and merchant trust. 

**[Method]** Utilizing a Technical Action Research approach and a Systematic Literature Review of existing RE4ML methodologies, we customized a prototype-driven, multi-phase requirements elicitation workflow involving sellers, admins, and data scientists. 

**[Key Findings]** Findings from the analyzed literature and experimental loops indicate that integrating live prototype demonstrations dynamically uncovers hidden data constraints and mitigates "pilot paralysis." Additionally, transforming qualitative explainability needs into quantitative technical features—such as Grad-CAM visualizations—significantly bridges the interdisciplinary communication gap and resolves accuracy-explainability trade-offs. 

**[Conclusion]** This research contributes an empirical validation of agile, prototype-driven RE in a highly sensitive public-facing domain, providing actionable guidance for building transparent and responsible e-commerce AI systems.

**Keywords:** Requirements Engineering, Machine Learning, E-Commerce, Product Categorization, Explainability, Prototype-Driven Elicitation.
